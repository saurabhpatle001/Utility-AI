UTILITY SENTINEL AI
UTILITY OPERATIONS STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES

1. SMART METER COMMUNICATION FAILURE

When a smart meter stops communicating or becomes offline, the field engineer should perform the following checks:

- Verify that the meter has electrical power.
- Check the meter communication status.
- Check the timestamp of the last successful meter reading.
- Inspect communication cables and physical connections.
- Verify network or communication connectivity.
- Check meter configuration and communication settings.
- Review recent communication error logs if available.
- If the communication failure persists after basic checks, escalate the incident for further technical investigation.

The system should not automatically disconnect or control the meter. Operational decisions must be made by qualified utility personnel.


2. CONSUMPTION SPIKE

When abnormal electricity consumption is detected, the operator should:

- Compare the current consumption with the meter's historical baseline.
- Verify the magnitude of the consumption deviation.
- Check whether a new or high-power load was recently connected.
- Inspect connected equipment for abnormal operation.
- Verify meter readings against a calibrated reference where available.
- Review recent meter events and operational logs.
- Check for possible meter or sensor faults.
- Investigate possible unauthorized or unexpected loads where appropriate.
- If the abnormal consumption persists, schedule a field inspection.

The operator should use actual meter readings and available historical information when evaluating the incident.


3. VOLTAGE ANOMALY

When abnormal voltage is detected:

- Verify the reported voltage reading.
- Compare the reading with recent historical measurements.
- Check whether the abnormal voltage is persistent or temporary.
- Inspect meter and electrical connections.
- Review available event or fault logs.
- Check upstream electrical equipment where appropriate.
- Escalate repeated or severe voltage anomalies to qualified utility personnel.

The system provides decision support and does not automatically control electrical equipment.


4. HIGH RISK INCIDENT

For a high-risk utility incident:

- Review the incident severity.
- Review the risk score.
- Examine the supporting meter evidence.
- Review historical consumption or electrical trends.
- Identify possible operational causes.
- Perform appropriate field or technical checks.
- Document the investigation result.
- Escalate the incident when required by operational procedures.

A high risk score indicates that an incident should receive higher investigation priority. It does not by itself prove the cause of the incident.


5. GENERAL OPERATIONAL PRINCIPLES

Utility Sentinel AI is a decision-support system.

AI-generated recommendations must be validated by qualified utility personnel before operational action.

The AI system must not invent sensor readings, measurements, events, or operational facts.

Recommendations should be based on available incident data and approved operational knowledge.

The system should assist operators in prioritizing and investigating incidents rather than automatically controlling utility infrastructure.
